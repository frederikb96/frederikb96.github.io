This page sets out the relevant clauses in full so that it can be shown to a conductor or attached to a passenger-rights complaint. The German original of this page is linked above; the German wording of every quoted clause is the binding one, and the English renderings below are working translations only.

## The situation

This arises on any journey with a connection and a tight transfer window:

- The ticket is a Sparpreis or Super Sparpreis, which in Germany is tied to specific trains (*Zugbindung*), with at least one change of train.
- The first train runs late. The app reports that the connection is no longer reachable and prompts the traveller to choose an alternative.
- The traveller picks an alternative in the app. From then on the app no longer displays the original connection and sends no further notifications about it. The display cannot be reverted; the original connection has to be searched for and saved again to become visible.
- The first train makes up part of the delay. The original connection would have been reachable after all at the interchange station. The traveller does not find out, because the app no longer carries it.
- The traveller continues on the alternative, possibly on a higher product class — an ICE instead of the booked IC.

## The question

Was the traveller obliged to check again, before changing trains, whether the originally booked connection had become reachable?

It matters in practice because the answer decides whether the journey on the alternative train was covered by the ticket. If it was not, the traveller is exposed to a surcharge for travelling without a valid ticket.

## Why no duty to re-check arises

### The right attaches to the forecast, not to what actually happened

Clause 9.1.1 of the conditions of carriage:

> Muss vernünftigerweise davon ausgegangen werden, dass der Reisende aufgrund einer Verspätung, eines verpassten Anschlusses oder eines Zugausfalls am Zielbahnhof gemäß Beförderungsvertrag mindestens 20 Minuten verspätet ankommen wird, hat er, auch mit einer zuggebundenen Fahrkarte, gegenüber dem EVU, dass die verspätete oder ausgefallene Beförderung vertraglich schuldet, unverzüglich die Wahl zwischen (i) der Fortsetzung der Fahrt oder der Weiterreise mit geänderter Streckenführung bis zum Zielbahnhof bei nächster Gelegenheit oder (ii) der Fortsetzung der Fahrt oder der Weiterreise mit geänderter Streckenführung bis zum Zielbahnhof zu einem späteren Zeitpunkt. Er kann dabei auch den Zug einer höherwertigen Produktklasse benutzen. Die Benutzung eines reservierungspflichtigen Zuges oder eines Sonderzuges ist allerdings nicht gestattet.
>
> *— Beförderungsbedingungen der DB AG, BB Personenverkehr clause 9.1.1 — [bahn.de/agb](https://www.bahn.de/agb)*

Working translation: where it must reasonably be assumed that the traveller will arrive at the destination station named in the contract of carriage at least 20 minutes late because of a delay, a missed connection or a cancellation, the traveller has, *even with a train-bound ticket*, an immediate choice between continuing the journey at the next opportunity, possibly by a different route, or continuing at a later time. A train of a higher product class may be used. Reservation-compulsory trains and special trains may not.

The trigger is the expectation, not the course events actually took. The choice exists *unverzüglich* — immediately, at the moment of knowledge. The clause contains no condition under which the choice lapses again.

### The app's own message is what makes the expectation reasonable

Clause 9.1.4 defines when the expectation counts as well-founded:

> Der Reisende kann insbesondere dann vernünftigerweise mit einer Verspätung nach Nr. 9.1.1 und Nr. 9.1.3 am Zielbahnhof rechnen, wenn diese über mindestens einen der nachfolgenden Informationskanäle bekanntgemacht wurde: (i) Aushangfahrpläne und ausgehängte Informationen über Fahrplanänderungen in Bahnhöfen, (ii) elektronische Anzeigen und Lautsprecheransagen in Zügen und auf Bahnhöfen, (iii) Fahrplaninformationen aus Buchungssystemen personalbedienter Verkaufsstellen sowie (iv) verfügbare Fahrplaninformations- und Reisendeninformationsmedien, insbesondere das Fahrplanauskunftssystem im Internet unter www.bahn.de. Die Übergangszeiten für planmäßige Umstiege (Umsteigezeiten) orientieren sich an der elektronischen Fahrplanauskunft unter www.bahn.de.
>
> *— Beförderungsbedingungen der DB AG, BB Personenverkehr clause 9.1.4 — [bahn.de/agb](https://www.bahn.de/agb)*

Working translation: the traveller may reasonably expect such a delay in particular where it has been announced through at least one of the following channels: posted timetables and posted information about timetable changes at stations; electronic displays and announcements on trains and at stations; timetable information from the booking systems of staffed sales points; and available timetable and passenger information media, in particular the online timetable information service at www.bahn.de. Transfer times for scheduled connections are governed by that same electronic timetable information service.

Deutsche Bahn thereby designates its own timetable information service as the authoritative channel, and the transfer times held in it as the yardstick for whether a connection counts as reachable. A traveller who relies on that information satisfies the condition in the clause exactly. No further duty of verification is imposed anywhere.

### Choosing an alternative does not change the ticket

From DB's own app documentation:

> Wichtig: Wenn Sie eine alternative Verbindung auswählen, behalten Sie Ihr ursprüngliches Ticket. Es werden ausschließlich die angezeigten Informationen im Reiseplan und die Benachrichtigungen zur Reise angepasst. Bitte prüfen Sie vorher, ob Ihr Ticket für die ausgewählte Alternative gültig ist.
>
> *— [bahn.de — DB Navigator, Funktionen](https://www.bahn.de/faq/pk/service/buchung/navigator/funktionen)*

Working translation: when you select an alternative connection you keep your original ticket. Only the information shown in the travel plan and the notifications for the journey are adjusted. Please check beforehand whether your ticket is valid on the selected alternative.

Selecting an alternative is a display setting. It creates no new train binding and removes no existing right. That the original connection disappears from view afterwards is a property of the app.

### The scope of the lifted binding argues against a same-day condition

From DB's guidance on lifted train binding:

> Sie können mit Ihrem ursprünglichen Ticket alle DB Fernverkehrszüge (ICE, IC/EC) und Nahverkehrszüge (RE, RB, IRE, S-Bahnen) nutzen. […] Sie können eine spätere Verbindung Ihrer Wahl nutzen (z.B. später am selben Reisetag, einen Tag später oder auch erst in 3 Wochen. Dies ist bis zu einem Jahr nach ursprünglichem Reisedatum möglich). […] Sie haben freie Wahl der Reiseroute. […] Innerhalb Deutschlands benötigen Sie keine zusätzliche Bescheinigung zur Aufhebung der Zugbindung. […] Hinweis: Bei Fahrkarten mit Zugbindung (z. B. Sparpreisen) ist diese automatisch aufgehoben.
>
> *— [bahn.de — In welchen Fällen ist die Zugbindung aufgehoben und was bedeutet das?](https://www.bahn.de/faq/zugbindung-aufgehoben-bedeutung)*

Working translation: the original ticket may be used on all DB long-distance trains (ICE, IC/EC) and regional trains (RE, RB, IRE, S-Bahn). A later connection of the traveller's choice may be used — later the same day, a day later, or three weeks later, up to one year after the original travel date. The route may be chosen freely. Within Germany no additional certificate confirming the lifted binding is required. For tickets with train binding, such as Sparpreis fares, the binding is lifted automatically.

A validity window of up to a year is hard to reconcile with the idea that the lifting could expire the same day, the moment the original train becomes reachable again.

## The counter-argument

It can be objected that clause 9.1.1 requires a forecast, and that a forecast must hold at the moment the decision is made. If the situation visibly improves before the decision is acted on, the forecast is superseded and the traveller should correct it.

The objection does not carry as far as a duty to re-check, for three reasons.

First, clause 9.1.1 grants the choice *unverzüglich*, at the moment of knowledge. The clause describes a point in time, not a state to be monitored continuously.

Second, clause 9.1.4 settles exhaustively what makes the expectation well-founded: announcement through one of the listed channels. No obligation to reconcile that announcement against later operating conditions is set out there.

Third, such an obligation would require the traveller to check the carrier's information against the carrier's own app, which stops displaying the original connection precisely once the alternative has been selected. An obligation that could only be discharged by working around the obligor's own user interface cannot be derived from this wording.

What remains correct in the objection is that the forecast must have been sound at the moment of decision. That is exactly what clause 9.1.4 supplies, by naming announcement through the timetable information service as a sufficient basis.

## Limits

- **Two categories of train are excluded.** Clause 9.1.1: "Die Benutzung eines reservierungspflichtigen Zuges oder eines Sonderzuges ist allerdings nicht gestattet" — reservation-compulsory trains and special trains may not be used. Domestic German ICE services are not reservation-compulsory.
- **Other operators are not covered.** The app occasionally offers connections run by other companies, such as Flixtrain, as alternatives. A DB ticket is not valid on those. That is what the validity warning in the app documentation is aimed at.
- **Not every such message lifts the binding.** The prompt to choose an alternative also appears for plain timetable changes that meet none of the conditions in clause 9.1.1. In those cases no right to a different train arises, and the app does not reliably distinguish the two cases in its display.
- **Regional tickets work differently.** A higher-class train has to be paid for first, with the cost then reclaimed from the passenger-rights service centre. For heavily discounted tickets such as the Deutschlandticket, even that does not apply.

## Evidence in practice

A screenshot of the message, taken **before** the alternative is selected, is the decisive piece of evidence. The message disappears from the app once the alternative has been adopted, and cannot be restored.

Within Germany no written certificate is required, though one can be issued on request on the train, at a travel centre or at a DB information desk.

## Standing of this analysis

The assessment rests on the wording of the conditions of carriage and the cited guidance pages. No published case law on this precise constellation is apparent. The conditions of carriage are reissued several times a year; the edition quoted is named above, and clause numbering may shift in later editions.

The underlying statutory framework is Regulation (EU) 2021/782 on rail passengers' rights and obligations, which clause 9.1.2 of the conditions of carriage itself refers to. The 20-minute threshold for domestic journeys is a contractual provision of Deutsche Bahn and goes beyond the Regulation.
