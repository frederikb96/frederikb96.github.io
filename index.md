## [Footprints and Handprints](/footprints-and-handprints/)

*Carbon and ecological accounting · whitepaper, PDF*

Why one country has several different and equally correct per-capita footprint figures, how a personal footprint is computed, where a product's system boundary falls, why the ecological footprint is mostly a carbon number wearing an area unit, and why a handprint can never be netted against a footprint.

## [Complete Food and Home Cooking](/complete-food-and-home-cooking/)

*Diet footprint and health evidence · whitepaper, PDF*

Vegan complete foods such as Huel, Jimmy Joy and Mana against a regional, seasonal, whole-food vegan diet in Germany. Both land around 1–3 kg CO₂e per 2000 kcal, the spread within each option is larger than the gap between them, and refinement rather than manufacturing drives the difference.
